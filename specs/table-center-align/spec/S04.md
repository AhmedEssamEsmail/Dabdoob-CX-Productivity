### Goal

### Files
- Edit: `src/app/cx-kit.css` | insert ONE rule immediately after line 660 (the numeric-cell `:is(td, tbody th):is(...)` rule), inside the same `/* --- header + numeric cell alignment --- */` block

### Context

Out of scope: thead cells, tbody th, chip component markup, any other cx-kit.css rule.

### Actions

### Done when

- Base `:is(th, td)` `text-align: start` and the `:is(td, tbody th):is(.cx-num, .num, .cx-num-col, .trk-num)` center rule are unchanged.
