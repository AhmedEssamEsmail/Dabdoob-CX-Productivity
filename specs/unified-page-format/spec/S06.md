Kind: package
ID: S06

## Goal and contract
All seven cx-people routes (`/agents`, `/coaching`, `/meetings`,
`/my-actions`, `/notes`, `/one-on-ones`, `/tl-tasks`) render the shared frame
through `PwShell` (`pw.tsx:127`) or `PageShell`. `PwShell` gains slot
passthroughs so most pages migrate by re-slotting props only. Content design
untouched; `.pw-page` marker stays. Non-goals: `.pw-page` min-height fix and
people `.main` geometry (S01-02 removed the override; S09-01 retargets the
min-height calc), `one-on-ones` view internals beyond their head props.
