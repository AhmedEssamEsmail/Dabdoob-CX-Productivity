CTLs — compact box options

Three layouts for the CTL list: compact boxes with all actions on one line. Rendered with the app's real stylesheets in dark theme — open a frame to view full-size.

A Single-line stripEverything on one row — score, name, ticket, owner, status pills, then buttons at the right edge. Densest option; long ticket refs truncate. Open → B Header + meta lineTwo lines per box: identity, pills and buttons share the top line; owner context sits on a quiet second line. Balanced density and readability. Open → C 2-col mini cardsSmall cards in a two-column grid — pills on top, ticket line, and a footer row holding owner text plus buttons on the same line. Open →
